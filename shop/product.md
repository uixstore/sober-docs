# Product Page

Product page is the page to present a single product information. You can controll the product page layout, style and other things in [Customize > Shop > Product](theme-options/shop.md?id=product) section. All options of this section are described before.


## Adding and Managing Products

You can easily found the detail tutorial for adding and managing product in the [WooComemrce Documentation](https://docs.woocommerce.com/document/managing-products/).


## Change Product Layout

You can select single product layout in **Customize > Shop > Product** with the option **Single Product Style**. There are 6 product layouts you can chose from:

1. Style 1 - [View example on demo](https://demo.uix.store/sober/product/hooded-coat/)
1. Style 2 - [View example on demo](https://demo.uix.store/sober/product/hooded-coat/?single_product_style=style-2)
1. Style 3 - [View example on demo](https://demo.uix.store/sober/product/lace-up-sneakers/?single_product_style=style-3)
1. Style 4 - [View example on demo](https://demo.uix.store/sober-furniture/product/iron-kettle-nambu-tekki/)
1. Style 5 - [View example on demo](https://demo.uix.store/sober-furniture/product/odin-chair/?single_product_style=style-5&product_related_carousel=1)
1. Style 6 - [View example on demo](https://demo.uix.store/sober-furniture/product/animal-box-jewellery-box/?single_product_style=style-6&product_related_carousel=1)


## Disable Product Sharing

You select disable product sharing by selecting no social in the option **Product Share** which is located in **Customize > Shop > Product**.


## Common Content After Every Product

If you want to display common content after the product short description of all products, Sober as the option **Extra Content** in **Customize > Shop > Product**. You can enter _HTML_ or _Shortcodes_ there.


## Ajax Add To Cart

By default, WooCommerce only has an option to enable ajax add to cart for products on the shop page. From version 1.1.3, Sober has a new option to enable ajax add to cart on single product page. Please navigate to **WooCommerce > Settings** and select tab **Product > Display**. You will see a new option **Enable AJAX add to cart button on single**.

## Add a video to product gallery

![Product options to add a video to gallery](_images/product--video-options.png#alignright)

From version 3.0, Sober added a new option that allow to add a video to the product gallery. You can add a video URL from Youtube, Video or a self-hosted video.

Besides, you are able to set the position of the video in the gallery. You can chose either "At the begining of the product gallery" or "At the end of the product gallery".
