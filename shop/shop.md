# Control Shop Display

When you go to **Appearance > Customize > Shop**, you will see three sections there: General, Catalog and Product.

The **Catalog** section contains options for customizing product archive pages, such as Shop, product category, product tag pages. All options are described in [Theme Options > Shop](theme-options/shop.md).


## Remove Shop Toolbar

You just need to turn off the **Shop Toolbar** option in **Customize > Shop > Catalog** to remove this toolbar from the shop page.


## Change Tabs

In **Customize > Shop > Catalog**, you will found the **Product Tabs Type** option that allows you to change these tabs. You can select to use group, product categories or product tags.

!> **Important** - The tabs of the shop toolbar is used to toggle products from "All Products" tab only. When you click on a tab, it will filter all products which are loaded in the "All Products" tab only. **It doesn't load products of selected tab**. Therefore sometime you won't see any product or only some products appear.


## Disable Quickview

You just need to turn off the **Product Quick View** option in **Customize > Shop > Catalog** to disable the quickview modal from your website.


## Change Product Columns

In **Customize > Shop > Catalog**, you will found the **Product Columns** option that allows you to change default product columns on the shop page.

!> **Important** - This is only the default columns will be used on your shop page. If you enable the shop toolbar, you will see it contains options that let visitors change the product columns by themself. After a visitor select a different columns layout, he/her selection will be stored. And on the next time he/she comeback to your website, the shop will use that stored selection to display your products.

![Shop Toolbar - Product Columns](_images/shop--toolbar.png)


## Change Product Rows

There is no option to change the product rows directly. Because it is calculated base on the number of product per page and the product columns option. For example, you select **5 Columns** as product column and **15 products per page**, it will display your products in 3 rows per page. To change it, please navigate to **Appearance > Customize > Shop > Catalog** and change **Product Columns** and **Products Per Page** options.
