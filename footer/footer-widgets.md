# Footer Widgets

Sober allows you to display widgets at footer in columns. All settiongs for footer widgets can be found in **Customize > Footer > Footer Widgets**.


## Enable Footer Widgets

By default, this footer widgets section is disabled. You need to enable it in **Customize > Footer > Footer Widgets**.


## Footer Widgets Columns

You can change the footer columns in **Customize > Footer > Footer Widgets**, from 2 to 4 columns.


## Add Widgets to Footer

After enabled Footer Widgets, you can navigate to **Appearance > Widgets** or **Appearance > Customize > Widgets** to add widgets to footer sidebars. They are sidebars:

- Footer Sidebar 1
- Footer Sidebar 2
- Footer Sidebar 3
